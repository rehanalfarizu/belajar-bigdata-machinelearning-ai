# Checkpoint — Math & Statistics

## Explain

- scalar/vector/matrix, dot/norm/transformation, eigen/SVD/PCA scope;
- function/derivative/gradient/chain rule/gradient descent;
- random variable, conditional/Bayes, expectation/variance;
- population/sample, sampling distribution, CLT/CI, hypothesis test, correlation/regression.

## Required evidence

- manual calculations checked by Python;
- foundation math lab dan tests lulus;
- four broken cases with prevention;
- Sensor Uncertainty Report;
- one reasoning problem Level 4/5;
- explanation of assumptions and failure boundaries.

## Oral/written challenge

Given rare failure alarm, explain base rate; given correlated readings, explain effective sample size; given correlation, refuse causal claim without design; given diverging loss, diagnose gradient/rate/scale.

## Gate

Lulus bila dapat memilih concept dari problem, menghitung contoh kecil, menginterpretasikan units/uncertainty, dan menyatakan kapan method tidak valid.
