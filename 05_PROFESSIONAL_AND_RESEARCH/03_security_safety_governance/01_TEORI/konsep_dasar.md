# Security, Safety, dan Governance

Security melindungi confidentiality, integrity, availability, identity, dan accountability. Safety mencegah unacceptable harm. Governance menentukan decision rights, ownership, evidence, review, exception, and lifecycle. Ketiganya beririsan tetapi tidak saling membuktikan.

Mulai dari asset dan data-flow diagram: actors, devices, workloads, stores, networks, trust boundaries, commands, model artifacts, and third parties. Threat model spoofing, tampering, replay, credential theft, poisoning/backdoor, denial of service, privilege escalation, and supply-chain compromise. Terapkan least privilege, workload/device identity, secret rotation, segmentation, encryption, allowlisted/versioned artifacts, secure update, audit, backup/recovery, dan incident response sesuai risk.

Safety analysis mendefinisikan hazard, severity, exposure, controllability, safe state, safety envelope, interlock, rate limit, watchdog, manual override, emergency handling, degraded mode, and recovery. Fail-safe bersifat domain-specific. Safety case menghubungkan claim→argument→evidence; ML metric saja tidak cukup untuk actuation.

AI/model governance menyimpan data/model provenance, intended use, limitations, evaluation per group/regime, approval, monitoring, change control, rollback, and retirement. Human-in-the-loop harus memiliki informasi, waktu, authority, dan interface yang benar; tombol approval kosmetik bukan safeguard.
