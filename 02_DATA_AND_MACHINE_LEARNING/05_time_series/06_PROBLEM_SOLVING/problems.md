# Time Series — Problem Ladder
## Level 1 — Recall
Definisikan event time, frequency, lag, trend, seasonality, horizon.
## Level 2 — Apply
Buat lag/rolling manual dan naive forecast.
## Level 3 — Analyze
Random split bagus, rolling deployment buruk. Temukan future leakage/drift.
## Level 4 — Design
Rancang multi-horizon rolling validation dan interval evaluation.
## Level 5 — Workplace
Sampling frequency dan retrain budget berubah. Redesign pipeline/policy.
