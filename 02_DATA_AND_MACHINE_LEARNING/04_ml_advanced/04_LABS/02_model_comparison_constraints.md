# Lab 02 — Model Comparison dengan Constraints
## 1. TUJUAN
Memilih model dari quality, uncertainty, latency, memory, dan maintainability.
## 2. PREREQUISITE
Lesson 01–05.
## 3. SETUP
Buat baseline, linear/tree, boosting candidate dan comparison table.
## 4. PREDICTION BEFORE RUN
Tetapkan acceptance tolerance serta constraint sebelum training.
## 5. LANGKAH PRAKTIKUM
Pipeline fold-local, CV sah, ablation, timing, size estimate, calibration/error slices.
## 6. OBSERVATION
Catat mean/spread metric dan operational costs.
## 7. WHY
Peningkatan kecil dapat kalah oleh latency/failure complexity.
## 8. MODIFICATION
Ubah false-negative atau latency budget.
## 9. FAILURE EXPERIMENT
Ranking hanya dari best fold/accuracy.
## 10. DEBUGGING
Recheck split, test contamination, paired folds, constraints, and reproducibility.
## 11. WORKPLACE CONNECTION
Muncul pada model review dan architecture decision record.
## 12. CHECKPOINT
Pertahankan pilihan, alternatif, trade-off, uncertainty, dan rejection reason.
