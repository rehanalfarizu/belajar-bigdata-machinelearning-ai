# Lab 01 — Manual Neuron dan Gradient
## 1. TUJUAN
Menelusuri forward, loss, gradient, dan update sebelum autograd.
## 2. PREREQUISITE
Lesson 01–02 dan `neural_mechanics.py`.
## 3. SETUP
Pilih dua inputs, weights, bias, target.
## 4. PREDICTION BEFORE RUN
Hitung score, sigmoid, loss, arah update manual.
## 5. LANGKAH PRAKTIKUM
Verifikasi code, finite difference, lalu train linear weight.
## 6. OBSERVATION
Catat gradient sign/norm dan loss curve.
## 7. WHY
Training mengubah parameter untuk menurunkan objective melalui chain rule.
## 8. MODIFICATION
Ubah rate/initial weight dan prediksi convergence.
## 9. FAILURE EXPERIMENT
Balik tanda update atau buat rate terlalu besar.
## 10. DEBUGGING
Tiny batch, shape check, gradient check, one-step loss comparison.
## 11. WORKPLACE CONNECTION
Muncul pada diagnosis divergence dan custom model/loss.
## 12. CHECKPOINT
Jelaskan satu update manual, cocokkan numeric gradient, break/fix training.
