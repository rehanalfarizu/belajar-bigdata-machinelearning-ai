# INTERVIEW-001 — Design dan Defend a Predictive Twin

## Prompt

Rancang predictive twin untuk 1.000 pumps dengan vibration/temperature/flow, maintenance labels tidak lengkap, dan connectivity terputus-putus. Tujuan bisnis: mengurangi unplanned downtime tanpa alarm berlebihan.

## Expected discussion

Tanyakan stakeholder/decision, failure criterion, data provenance, event/state semantics, baseline, simulation/ML role, validation split, uncertainty, detection-delay/false-alarm metrics, architecture, capacity, observability, security/safety boundary, human workflow, rollout, monitoring, rollback, dan limitations.

Interviewer akan mengubah satu constraint di tengah sesi. Jangan menghafal stack; tunjukkan bagaimana design berubah dan apa yang tetap invariant.
